Since 1998, one campus has turned into a laboratory, a stage and an arena every December. Students build. Machines fight. Ideas collide. This year the question is simple: what if the next renaissance starts with you?

III · The Codex

Twenty-eight years in the margins.

Drag or scroll sideways through the notebook.

1998

The first spark

Students at IIT Bombay launch Techfest with competitions, lectures, workshops and exhibitions — the core that endures.

2006

Applied technology

The focus shifts from pure engineering toward what technology can actually do for people.

2009

Scintillations

Night exhibitions and interactive shows arrive, lighting up the campus after dark.

2015

1.65 lakh souls

A then-record footfall of 1.65 lakh visitors across three days.

2018

A Guinness record

Most LED lights lit simultaneously — a world record set on campus.

2019

Da Vincian Spectacle

Art and science fuse under a Renaissance banner — the ancestor of this year's theme.

2022

India's first 5G campus
