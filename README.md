 #Techfest 2026 — An Aetherial Renaissance (landing page concept)

A concept landing page for [Techfest, IIT Bombay](https://techfest.org) — the 30th edition, 16–18 December 2026.
Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. **No 3D assets, no frameworks, no build step.**

## Concept

The 2026 theme, *An Aetherial Renaissance*, fuses old-master curiosity with future tech. The page leans on that:

- **Aether field** — a 2D canvas constellation of drifting particles that is drawn toward your cursor.
- **Orrery hero** — slowly counter-rotating SVG rings, tick marks and geometric frames (flat line art, not 3D).
- **Live countdown** to 16 Dec 2026, 00:00 IST.
- **Six Pillars** — Competitions, Lectures, Exhibitions, Workshops, Robowars, Technoholix, with cursor-following spotlight cards.
- **Legacy stats** — count-up numbers (180,000+ footfall, 2,500+ Indian colleges, 500+ international universities, and more).
- **The Codex** — a draggable horizontal timeline from 1998 to 2026.
- **Visit** — dates, venue, hostel stay, Catalyst seed fund, CES 2027 partnership.

**Interaction layer**

- **Custom cursor** — a dot plus a trailing ring that grows over links and shows labels ("Explore", "Drag") on cards and the timeline.
- **Comet trail and sparks** that follow the pointer; **click bursts** with ripples and a shockwave that pushes the star field.
- **Reactive headline** — each letter of the hero title repels and glows as the cursor nears.
- **Constellation threads** from the cursor to nearby stars, **magnetic buttons**, icon parallax on cards, and a cursor glow that changes colour per section.
- **Intro loader**, scroll progress bar, scroll-lit manifesto text, text-scramble labels, and a marquee that speeds up as you scroll.
- **"Find your pillar" picker** that highlights the cards matching what you want to do.
